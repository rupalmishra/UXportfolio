Hi, I’m Rupal

I work on ECAP, Escriba’s platform for managing files and workflows in large companies. Most of what I design is already live, people are using it every day, so I have to be careful with every change I make.

I like working with the messy stuff: old systems nobody wants to touch, data that’s survived a few redesigns already, and people who just want their tools to work, not get reinvented every year.

Before I open Figma, I talk to the people who actually use the tool and figure out how the work happens. The screens come after that.
